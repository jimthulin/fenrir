The Codex — strategy definitioncomplete rules · click to expand
Position rule — evaluated every Friday close, traded Monday

1. If both machines read BEAR (consensus leverage −2) → Bear ×2 — XACT Bear 2 (SE0005466505)

2. Else if OMXS30 close > MA52w → Bull ×2 — XACT Bull 2 (SE0003051010)

3. Else → Bull ×1 — plain OMXS30 ETF. Fenrir never holds cash.

The ladder is the base: full leverage above trend, half below, never out. The override is rare by design — the wolf is loosed only when two independent machines agree.

MSCI World deployment — identical rules on the World index: ×2 via Amundi MSCI World 2x Lev (LVWC, FR0014010HV4, TER 0.60%), ×1 via any plain World ETF, Bear via a −2x S&P 500 proxy (no inverse World UCITS exists). Panel backtest 16.5%/yr, −62% MDD, Sharpe 0.74 — out-of-sample for every threshold.

The machines — identical V8b++ rules on OMXS30 and S&P 500

Bull ×2 (RSI-entered) → Cash on RSI-14 ≥ 69

Bull-TR (trend-entered) → Cash on RSI ≥ 69 or close < MA40w

Cash → Bull on RSI ≤ 35 and MACD% > −2  ·  → Bear ×2 on Mom4w < −4% and close < MA40w  ·  → Bull-TR on close > MA40w and RSI 40–60

Bear ×2 → Bull on RSI ≤ 35 and MACD% > −2  ·  → Cash on close > MA40w

Priority within each state is top-to-bottom as listed. A blocked entry dies — no pending signals. Only the BEAR state matters to Fenrir; everything else is the arming sequence that makes BEAR rare.

Indicator definitions

RSI-14 — Wilder smoothing on weekly % changes, seeded with a simple 14-week average. Equivalently: price's distance from its 14w Wilder EMA in units of average weekly move — RSI 35 ≈ 3.9 units below, RSI 69 ≈ 4.9 above.

MACD% — (EMA12w − EMA20w) / close × 100. The −2 gate measures downtrend depth at the oversold moment; deep MACD blocks knife-catching entries and keeps shorts running in grinds.

MA40w / MA52w — simple averages of weekly closes. MA40w (≈ MA200 daily) anchors the machines; MA52w anchors the ladder.

Mom4w — 4-week price change. The collapse detector: fires within weeks of a top, which is the only place a short earns its risk.

Consensus — average of the two machines' leverages (Bull/Bull-TR = +2, Cash = 0, Bear = −2). Fenrir reads only the −2 reading.

Provenance & caveats

Backtest (Oct 1986 – Apr 2026, 0.15%/switch): 19.3%/yr · −69% MDD · Sharpe 0.70 · beats ×1 B&H in 99.9% of 10-year windows, ×2 B&H in 85%.

Honest expectation: thresholds were set in-sample on this exact path; walk-forward testing halves the machine excess and the cross-index panel (DAX +6.2pp, Nasdaq +5.5, Nikkei +4.7, MSCI World +2.5, FTSE −3.0) puts the override's transferable kicker at 1–2pp. Expect live nearer 14–16%/yr. The −69% drawdown is real and arrives while ×1 B&H is down ~70% alongside — Fenrir is built to beat the index through cycles, not to avoid bear-market pain.

Design grammar (the part that generalizes): a one-parameter structural base that nearly dominates ×1 by construction · an armed sequence (euphoric top → momentum collapse) rather than raw momentum · a dual-market veto that removes ~90% of false bear flags · a regime-aware short cover via the MACD gate.

The Saga — forty years of the wolfreal-world triggers · strategy response · numbers computed live from the record
1986 — 1988

1987 · BLACK MONDAY

October 19, 1987: portfolio insurance and program trading turn a correction into the largest one-day crash in modern history. Stockholm, five hours ahead of New York, absorbs it across that whole week.
The wolf rode ×2 into the crash — no signal is fast enough for a one-day event, and Fenrir doesn't pretend otherwise. The MA52 break stepped it to ×1 in the aftermath, and the 1988 recovery reclaimed the trend for ×2. The founding lesson: fast crashes are absorbed at reduced strength, never dodged.
—
1990 — 1993

1990–93 · THE SWEDISH BANKING CRISIS

A burst property bubble takes down Första Sparbanken, Nordbanken and Gota Bank. September 1992: the Riksbank defends the krona with a 500% marginal rate, and on November 19 the peg falls and the krona floats.
The longest chained stretch in the record — ×1 for most of three years. The override never fired because Wall Street wasn't in a synchronized bear; this was Sweden's own crisis, and the dual veto held its fire, correctly by the panel evidence. The ×1 rung did the quiet work the Nikkei test later confirmed: half strength through a grind still compounds. The krona float lit the fuse of the 90s bull, and the wolf went ×2 into it.

2000 — 2003

2000–03 · DOTCOM — THE FIRST RAGNARÖK

The March 2000 Nasdaq top. Stockholm's exposure is extreme — Ericsson alone is a third of the index — and the decline runs 30 months, punctuated by vicious bear-market rallies.
The defining episode. Euphoric spring top armed both machines; by October 2000 both read BEAR and the wolf was loosed for the first time — five months short through the first collapse leg, the largest single contribution to Fenrir's lifetime edge over ×2 buy & hold. Then chained at ×1 through the grind while ×2 B&H lost 93% and needed until the 2020s to recover. The MA52 reclaim in spring 2003 sent it back to full strength near the bottom.

2007 — 2009

2007–09 · GLOBAL FINANCIAL CRISIS

August 2007: the quant quake and BNP Paribas freezing funds. Autumn: Bear Stearns wobbles, SocGen unwinds. September 2008: Lehman. March 2009: the bottom.
Three short bursts — Aug 2007, Nov 2007, Jan 2008 — as the machines caught the topping process in real time. The shorts expired before Lehman, so the autumn was ridden at ×1: painful, roughly half the index's leveraged loss, and the honest reminder that the override catches tops, not every leg down. Mid-2009 reclaim → ×2 into the recovery.

2010 — 2019

2010–19 · THE LONG BULL, WITH TREMORS

Euro crisis 2011, the October 2014 growth scare, China's August 2015 devaluation, the 2015–16 industrial recession, the Q4 2018 rate tantrum.
Mostly ×2, the decade Fenrir is built to fully harvest. The tremors show the machinery breathing: single-week flags in Oct 2014 and Aug 2015 (both machines briefly aligned), and ×1 stints in 2011, 2015–16 and late 2018 as the MA52 broke and recovered. Every dip resolved fast — the modern regime in miniature.

2020 — 2021

2020 · COVID — THE PERFECT STRIKE

February 24–28, 2020: the fastest 30% decline in market history begins as northern Italy locks down. March 23: the bottom. Then the greatest stimulus-driven rally on record.
The design's masterpiece. Both machines flipped BEAR on February 28 — the exact week the crash began — and the wolf was short through the collapse itself. The gated RSI covered near the bottom in mid-March (shallow MACD = V-crash = cover), the ladder held ×1 through the chop, and the MA52 reclaim caught the melt-up at ×2. Signal, veto and gate each did precisely their job within four weeks.

2022 · INFLATION AND WAR

CPI prints not seen in forty years, the Fed's fastest hiking cycle since Volcker, and Russia's February invasion of Ukraine.
The override fired in the top weeks of January–February 2022, shorting the break, then chained ×1 through the down year while ×2 B&H bled. The 2023 reclaim restored full strength. A clean mid-size demonstration: top caught, grind endured at half strength, recovery harvested.
